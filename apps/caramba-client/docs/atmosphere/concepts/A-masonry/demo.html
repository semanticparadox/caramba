Atmosphere concept A

Masonry

The background is a wall. Disconnected, it is a closed course of panels pressed flat against the glass with the corners dimmed. Connected, the same panels retreat into their corners and the wall becomes an open lattice with depth and light behind it. Same geometry, one parameter.

Opening is centrifugal, closing is centripetal. Connect runs a single wavefront out from the dial to the frame edge and leaves every cell it passes open. Disconnect runs a closing front inward, so the last cell to seal is the one under your thumb.

Connecting is a wall that will not stay open. A wave opens outward and a second wave seals it shut behind, on a 1.9 s loop. It reads as a handshake being retried, and it is the only state that animates continuously.

Connected is quiet. Static frame at 0 fps. Every 4.8 s a reach pulse leaves the dial and crosses the lattice in 1.6 s, then the canvas goes idle again. Watch the repaint readout above.

Hue is confirmation, not signal. Status colour is a mix of at most 16% on strut and node strokes, never on fills. Meaning is carried by whether the wall is closed or open. The atmoGuard layer damps the atmosphere behind the dial and its labels so text contrast never depends on the state.
