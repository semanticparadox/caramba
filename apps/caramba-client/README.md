<h1 align="center">Caramba Connect</h1>
<p align="center">Your connection. Your course.</p>

A VPN app for connecting with your provider's subscription, with dedicated mobile and desktop interfaces.

## Download

Choose a **Caramba Connect** build from [Releases](https://github.com/semanticparadox/caramba/releases), follow its installation notes, and import your provider's subscription.

Packages are published for Android, macOS, Windows and Linux. Caramba Connect is in beta; platform capabilities and signing vary. iOS is not available for public installation yet.
