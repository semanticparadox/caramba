## Proposed direction

A substantial two-masted ship represents a refuge of freedom on the internet.
The product mark uses a strong monochrome silhouette. Repository artwork uses
nautical engraving and interrupted chart boundaries. The existing application
palette stays neutral; green, amber and red retain their status meanings.

## Prompt set

**Cover:** Premium wide 3:1 GitHub cover for Caramba, internet freedom software.
Monochrome charcoal and ivory, contemporary editorial engraving with precise
nautical chart linework. Powerful two-masted blockade-running ship, substantial
angular hull, tall square sails, moving from dark hatched waters into open sea.
Ship on right, quiet dark negative space on left for real typography. No text,
watermark, skulls, weapons, cartoon pirates, padlocks, neon or 3D mockups.

**Mark:** Single black and white Caramba Connect ship symbol. Substantial hull,
two masts, staggered square sails and a tiny pennant. Simplified geometric masses,
precision identity design and nautical woodcut influences. Strong silhouette and
wide negative-space separations, no hairline rigging, waves, shield, border or
text. Intended for recognition at 24 px and 1024 px. Requested white background.

**App icon:** Use the generated ship as identity reference. Flat square icon,
near-black edge-to-edge background, centered off-white ship with generous margins.
Simplify to two major sails, preserve angular hull and staggered masts. Wide gaps,
no hairlines, text, baked-in rounded corners, frame, gloss or perspective tile.
Intended to remain recognizable at 32 px. The current raster is a concept and
has not yet been certified against the exact two-color production requirement.
